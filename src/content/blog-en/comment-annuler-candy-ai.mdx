To **cancel Candy AI**, check the current account and billing instructions before the renewal date. Cancellation generally concerns recurring billing; it does not automatically mean that an account or its data is deleted.

Read our [Candy AI review](/en/ai-companions/candy-ai-review-2026/) and [privacy guide](/en/guides/ai-girlfriend-privacy/) for related checks.

## Before and after cancellation

Confirm the next billing date, any remaining access and the confirmation supplied by the service. Keep a record of the action where appropriate.
