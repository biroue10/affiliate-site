An **AI girlfriend** must process what you send in order to reply. That does not make every chat public, but it does mean an online service should not be confused with an encrypted diary kept only on your device.

## What to read in a policy

Check processing purposes, retention, deletion, service providers and moderation. Do not send passwords, banking data, identity documents or sensitive third-party information.

Our [Candy AI review](/en/ai-companions/candy-ai-review-2026/), [OurDream review](/en/ai-companions/ourdream-review-2026/) and [Lovescape review](/en/ai-companions/lovescape-review-2026/) provide platform context.

## Deletion is not always immediate or absolute
