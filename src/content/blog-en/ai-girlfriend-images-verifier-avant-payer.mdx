Before paying for an **AI girlfriend image** feature, distinguish the advertised subscription from what it actually includes. Check how many images or edits are covered, whether credits expire, and whether the service limits styles, characters or reference images.

For platform context, read our [Candy AI review](/en/ai-companions/candy-ai-review-2026/), [OurDream review](/en/ai-companions/ourdream-review-2026/) and [Lovescape review](/en/ai-companions/lovescape-review-2026/).

## Check costs before you commit

An initial price does not reveal the cost of repeated generations. Read the payment screen, any credit schedule and the limits that apply to images, retouches or higher-resolution outputs.

## Check privacy and rights

Read the terms governing uploaded references, generated material, moderation and deletion. Do not upload images of another person without permission. Our [privacy guide](/en/guides/ai-girlfriend-privacy/) provides a wider checklist for sensitive information.

## Keep expectations realistic

Product capabilities and restrictions change. Use only the information displayed by the service at the moment you pay, and avoid assuming an image option grants unrestricted use.
