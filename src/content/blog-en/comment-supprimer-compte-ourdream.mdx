Deleting an **OurDream account** is not the same as stopping a subscription. This guide relies on official information checked on **21 August 2026**; screens and labels can change.

Read our [OurDream review](/en/ai-companions/ourdream-review-2026/) for product context and our guide on [whether OurDream is safe](/en/guides/is-ourdream-safe-2026/) for data and privacy considerations.

## Delete the account, not only the subscription

First identify whether you are ending recurring billing, deleting the account or both. Check the confirmation supplied by the service and the remaining access associated with a paid period.

## Check data and retention limits

The policy may distinguish active-system deletion from backups, legal retention or public content. Our [AI companion privacy guide](/en/guides/ai-girlfriend-privacy/) explains the questions worth asking before you remove an account.
