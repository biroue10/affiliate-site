A **dating site without a subscription** can mean free access, a trial, credits or one-off payments. The useful question is what you can actually do before paying.

## Check the payment model

Verify the total price, currency, taxes, duration, renewal, included credits and cancellation instructions. A trial can become paid automatically.

See our [discreet dating guide](/en/adult-dating/discreet-dating-sites/) and [dating without required photos guide](/en/adult-dating/dating-sites-without-required-photos/) for the surrounding privacy considerations.

## Avoid surprises

Keep a record of the offer shown to you and read terms that use labels such as access, trial, premium or credits without clearly stating what they cover.
