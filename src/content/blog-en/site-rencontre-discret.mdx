A **discreet dating site** should let you understand profile visibility, photo sharing, payment and deletion before you publish personal information.

## Prioritise controls over promises

Choose a pseudonym, use a dedicated email address and review every visible field. Check blocking, reporting, support and account deletion before you need them.

Read our guides to [dating without required photos](/en/adult-dating/dating-sites-without-required-photos/) and [dating without a subscription](/en/adult-dating/dating-sites-without-subscription/) for related choices.

## Protect everyday privacy

Avoid combining details that identify your workplace, routine or location. Private browsing can reduce local traces but does not hide activity from a platform or network.
